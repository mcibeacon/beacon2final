**There is no better way to welcome the fall season than by baking cinnamon rolls.** When autumn weather turns chilly, nothing beats that warm blend of sweet and salty aromas filling your home. It is also the perfect indoor hobby for relaxing on the weekends. You don't need to be experienced to bake these gooey treats. Using basic ingredients that you already have at home, you will be able to easily whip up a batch. Follow these steps to bake your own classic autumn dessert.

  

Ingredients:

  

For the dough:

  

- Flour: 2 ¾ cups, all-purpose

  

- Sugar: ¼ cup

  

- Salt: ½ teaspoon

  

- Yeast: 1 packet of instant yeast

  

- Milk: ¾ cup, warmed

  

- Butter: 3 tablespoons unsalted butter, melted

  

- Egg: 1 large 

  
  

For the filling:

  

- Butter: 3 tablespoons unsalted butter, 

  

- Brown sugar: ⅓ cup light brown sugar

  

- Cinnamon: 1 tablespoon ground cinnamon

  
  

For the glaze:

  

- Cream cheese: 4 ounces cream cheese, softened

  

- Powdered sugar: ¾ cup

  

- Vanilla: 1 teaspoon vanilla extract

  
  

Instructions:

  

1. Mix the dough: Whisk the flour, sugar, salt, and instant yeast together in a large mixing bowl. Pour in the warm milk, melted butter, and egg, stirring until a soft dough forms.

  

2. Knead and rest: Turn the dough out onto a lightly floured surface and knead by hand for 5 minutes until smooth. Cover the dough with a clean towel and let rest for 10 minutes.

  

3. Roll and fill: Use a rolling pin to roll the dough into a large rectangle. Spread the softened butter evenly across the surface, then sprinkle the brown sugar and cinnamon over the top.

  

4. Cut and rise: Roll the dough tightly into a log shape and slice it into 10 even rounds. Place the rolls into a greased baking pan, cover them, and let them rise in a warm spot for 60 minutes until doubled in size.

  

5. Bake and frost: Bake at 375°F (190°C) for 25 minutes until golden brown. Whisk the cream cheese, powdered sugar, and vanilla together, then spread the icing over the warm rolls before serving.

  

The result will taste better than anything you can buy at a store!
