**Now that fall sports have started at Martingrove, our students are competing across various sports such as volleyball, Girls’ Ultimate Frisbee, XC, and tennis.** But what if Halloween's most influential characters joined our teams? Could they give us a season to remember?

  

On the volleyball court, standing as tall as a tree, Frankenstein would be my pick to play middle blocker. His height alone would make blocking shots instinctive. Sadly, his coordination might need some help. Watching him try to spike or serve the ball could be scarier than Halloween itself!

  

A couple of kilometers away, at Centennial Park, the Werewolf would dominate cross country. With its brawny legs and the intensity of its strides, qualifying for OFSAA would be no big deal for this creature of the night. The only problem? The Werewolf would rather chase its fellow runners than bring home gold for Martingrove.

  

Spider-Man would excel for us in tennis. His exceptional reflexes, lightning-fast pace and spidey sense could even give prime Djokovic a run for his money. However, TDSSAA might have questions if he starts using his webs to rally rather than his racket!

  

Last but not least, Catwoman would most definitely do well in Girls’ Ultimate. Her agility and tenacity would allow her to catch the disc at any height and speed over the opponents’ endzone. Let’s just hope that she doesn't take any rival’s fouls purr-sonally.

  

Together, this lineup would strike fear in the hearts of our opponents, even more so than we do now. Best of luck this season Bears!
