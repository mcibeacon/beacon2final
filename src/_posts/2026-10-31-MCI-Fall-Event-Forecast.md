**Fall—what an exciting time!** Beautifully coloured leaves, crisp weather and spooky seasons are here. I know for sure that it’s going to be a very vibrant and busy autumn at MCI this year. Our amazing students and staff have a stellar lineup of fall activities that we’re sure you’ll enjoy. From athletic games to spooky haunts, we truly have it all. If you want to learn more about these fantastic events (and convince yourself to attend), keep reading!

  

First up, we’re starting strong this November with the highly anticipated BAC Pep Rally! Each fall team will be doing a walkout and the seasonal highlight video will be played. Then, the most exciting part—games! I know what we’re all wondering: what are the teams this season? MCI is competing in Golf, Cross Country, Boys Volleyball, Varsity Girls Basketball, Varsity Girls Ultimate, and Fall Tennis! Make sure to show up with maximum energy - our athletes need their fans, and that’s exactly what being a Bear is all about!

  

Next up, we have SACs’ fun and exciting Halloween events! Do you like haunted houses or just want to have a bewitching time with your friends? If so, prepare yourself for a spooky haunted house that will definitely give you a scare. So, bring your friends for the perfect mix of fun and fright. SAC is also holding a very exciting costume contest where the best dressed student will receive a gift card! Also, look out for upcoming spirit week announcements!

  

I highly recommend attending the exciting fall events at MCI. They offer a much-needed break from your heavy workload and stress, and allow you to enjoy high school life. If you're new to Martingrove, partaking in these events can boost your school spirit and help you get to know our community. MCI events have such a kind and welcoming atmosphere, so don’t be afraid to join with your friends, make new ones, or both. High school flies by and activities like this are where core memories and lifelong friendships are created. Make sure that you listen closely to the announcements every morning so that you don’t miss out on any of the action! Come out and unleash your inner MCI Bear!
