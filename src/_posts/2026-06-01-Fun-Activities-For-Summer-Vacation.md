With the school year coming to a close, many people are looking forward to summer vacation and getting a break from school. However it can be hard to find ways to spend all your extra time, especially if you don’t have anything planned. If you are bored and are not sure how to fill your days, here are plenty of classic summer activities you can do instead of scrolling.

  

When most people think of summer, they think of going to the beach. It is a popular summer activity, and for good reason. Going to the beach is a great way to spend time with your family and friends, and be in nature. There is something for everyone at the beach: you can swim, surf, or hang out with your friends. If you do not like water, you could relax on the sand, make sand castles, search for shells, or play games like volleyball.

  

Many beaches also have refreshments such as ice cream or fruit juice so that you can stay cool. This makes going to the beach an enjoyable summer activity for everyone. Just make sure you bring sunscreen!

  

Another great place to go in the summer is an amusement park like *Canada’s Wonderland*. This is a great idea because there are many fun rides and games for all types of people. There are plenty of thrilling roller coaster rides, if you find them fun. However, there are also other rides that are slower, such as carousels, bumper cars, or Ferris wheels. There are also gift shops where you can get souvenirs to help you remember your fun day, and countless options for good food, so you can enjoy anything you want. On the theme of amusement parks, you can also visit water parks like Wet‘n’Wild, which have fun water rides that can be refreshing when it is very hot outside. Since amusement parks are so versatile, they can be a good choice for anyone on a boring summer day.

  

Lastly, going on a picnic is also a nice summer activity. Even though it is simple, it can still be a very fun way to spend your time. You can invite your friends or family and just relax, enjoying delicious food. It is also a very flexible activity because you can do any number of things at a picnic such as reading, playing board games, or just chatting. You can also choose any picnic location you want, but popular spots in Toronto include Centennial Park, High Park, and anywhere along the lakeshore. Moreover, picnics can be a fairly inexpensive activity, especially if everyone contributes some food for the picnic. This makes it an affordable option if you do not want to spend a lot, but want to get a big return on fun and memories.

  

Overall, there are plenty of fun and engaging activities that you can do to spend your summer vacation in a fun way, including catching some sun at the beach, going to an amusement park or even a simple picnic. Most importantly, though, make sure you take some friends or family with you so you make memories that will last!
